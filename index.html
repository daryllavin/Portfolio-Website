About Me

I love to learn new things and understand the world around us. I have taken coursework from cloud computing and machine learning down through computer systems/architecture, all the way to analog and microelectronic circuits. I'm especially drawn to low-level systems and the interconnection between hardware and software.

In the past, I was a software engineer intern at Aven Financial, a $2.2 billion fintech startup, and a research intern at the Yale Biomedical Imaging Institute. I'm currently a teaching assistant for 15-440/15-640: Distributed Systems, and previously was a TA for 18-213/18-613: Computer Systems, CMU's core software systems course. Check out the projects page to see some cool stuff I've worked on.

I also love to play the trombone. I started playing in fifth grade and now spend 10-20 hours a week on music. I'm pursuing an additional major in Trombone Performance at CMU, where I study with the principal trombonist of the Pittsburgh Symphony, and I perform in ensembles at CMU and in Connecticut. See more on the music page.

Outside of school, I have played hockey since I was six, love to go for long walks and travel, and play the occasional game of chess. I also love Pittsburgh.
