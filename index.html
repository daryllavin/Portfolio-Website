About

I love to learn new things and understand the world around us. I have taken coursework from cloud computing and machine learning down through operating systems and computer architecture, all the way to analog and microelectronic circuits. I have been especially interested by computer architecture, systems programming, and machine learning.

In the past, I've interned at Aven Financial, a $2.2 billion fintech startup, and at the Yale Biomedical Imaging Institute. Right now I'm a teaching assistant for 15-440/15-640: Distributed Systems, and before that I was a TA for 18-213/18-613: Computer Systems, CMU's core systems course. Some of the things I've built are on the projects page.

Outside of engineering, I play trombone. I started in fifth grade and now spend 10–20 hours a week on music. I'm pursuing an additional major in Trombone Performance at CMU, where I study with the principal trombonist of the Pittsburgh Symphony, and I perform in ensembles at CMU and back home in Connecticut. There's more on the music page.

Outside of school, I have played hockey since I was six, love to go for long walks, travel, or play the occasional game of chess.
