Music

I'm a trombonist in the Carnegie Mellon Trombone Studio, where I study with Pete Sullivan, principal trombone of the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra. I grew up in New Canaan, CT, studying with George Sanders, retired associate principal trombone of the Hartford Symphony.

Email me

Featured performance

Holst's Second Suite in F, which I played as first trombone with the CMU Wind Ensemble.

Background

In high school I was the low brass section leader and drum major of my school band, and played first chair in the regional honors concert and jazz bands. I made the Connecticut All-State Band twice and played first trombone in the NAfME All-Eastern and All-National Symphony Orchestras.

I've also played around southwestern Connecticut with the Western CT Youth Orchestra and the Norwalk Youth Symphony, which gave me a full-tuition scholarship. I still play with the New Canaan Town Community Band at town events like fireworks and Christmas carols.

During the school year I'm in Pittsburgh, playing in the CMU brass ensemble, Philharmonic, and Wind Ensemble. I'm always happy to play, so feel free to reach out.

Timeline
